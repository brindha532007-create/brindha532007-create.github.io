Week 0: Orientation & Team Foundations

Inauguration Ceremony, Strengths Discovery & Rapid Prototyping Kickoff

WEEK 0 CONTENT & FIELD REFLECTIONS

Week 0 of PRICE Protosem was all about getting started, connecting with our peers, and building a strong foundation for the journey ahead. We participated in the inauguration ceremony, team introductions, personality tests, storytelling, and the Marshmallow & Spaghetti challenge.

These activities helped us understand our teammates’ strengths, personalities, creativity, communication, and teamwork, while bringing together students from diverse academic backgrounds.

✨ A great beginning to an exciting 20-week journey of learning, collaboration, and solving real-world retail challenges!

Core Week 0 Highlights

Inauguration Ceremony

Official induction into the PRICE ProtoSem 20-week fellowship, setting the stage for problem-solving across retail, AI, and IoT.

Team Introductions & Personality Tests

Mapped communication styles, individual strengths, and interpersonal synergy across fellow innovators.

Creative Storytelling

Practicing expressive articulation, sharing vision narratives, and active listening within multidisciplinary groups.

Marshmallow & Spaghetti Challenge

Hands-on structural rapid prototyping exercise to test agile iterative development and time-critical teamwork.
